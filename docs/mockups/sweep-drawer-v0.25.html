Why today's drawer feels empty: it opens to a fixed 340 px whatever the tab holds, its fields don't stretch (the Domain tab is one dropdown across 1,400 px), there is no help text under the fields the way Lab has, and the tab rail runs well past its six tabs. Both options below fit the drawer to its content and fill the width. Shown at 1920 × 1080.

Same six tabs, but each tab is one row of fields spread across the full width, every field with its help line underneath, and the drawer only as tall as that row (216 px instead of 340). 2 — everything at a glance. Settings, Log and Metric key become three tabs along the top; Settings shows all four groups side by side — Recipe & sweep · Material · Solver · Ranks — so one click shows (and changes) the whole sweep.
