F13LD.sweep · Cell scale visual

Five smaller ways to show which cells a sweep can draw, each in the real column at its real width. Change the ranges or switch to per axis below, and every option redraws. "Most stretched" is the longest high end over the shortest low end on two different axes; that is also the voxel aspect the solver sees, because the grid stays N × N × N.
